Adding REAL ID To add the REAL ID indicator to your Kansas credential, you will need to present the following documents. 1.) You will need to provide one lawful presence document listed below. State Issued Birth Certificate Unexpired Passport Unexpired Permanent Resident Card Unexpired Employment Authorization Card Naturalization Certificate Consular Report of Birth Abroad (FS-240) If you are a foreign student, you will also need to present your I-20 or DS-2019 2.) You must provide proof of your Social Security Number. Please select ONE document from the list below: Social Security Card Current W-2 or 1099 showing full Social Security Number Current pay stub showing full Social Security Number If you are a foreign student or dependent in the U.S. on a non-work authorized status, you will not need to provide SSN verification if you have not been issued a social security number. 3.) You must provide two recent proofs of current Kansas residential address. Proof must display your name, and show physical address, not a separate mailing or PO box only. Examples are listed below USPS Change of Address Utility Bill Bank statement or letter from financial institution Cancelled mail Lease agreement Title documents Insurance documents 4.) If your current legal name does not match your lawful presence document, you will need to provide your legal name change document. Certified State Issued Marriage Certificate Court Ordered Divorce Decree Any Court Ordered or Legal Name Change Documents Legal Adoption Paperwork 5.) If you are a military veteran and would like the word “Veteran” printed on your credential, please present one document below. DD-214 stating Honorable Discharge or Under Honorable Conditions NGB-22 that states Honorable Discharge or Under Honorable Conditions VA Card Retired Military ID Please note: Documents cannot be photocopied or laminated. You can print our portable checklist to make sure you have all the required documents when you come to the driver's licensing office. Have all required documents?

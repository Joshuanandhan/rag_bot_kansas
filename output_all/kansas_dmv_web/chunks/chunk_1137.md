Driving Records in Tennessee Driving Records in Tennessee Driving records, also called motor vehicle records (MVRs), are official documents that contain your driving history. In Tennessee, MVRs are provided by the TN Department of Safety and Homeland Security. Some of the most popular reasons to order a driving record are: To prevent identity theft. To conduct background checks on a potential employee. For peace of mind and making sure your driving record is correct. For insurance purposes. You may be able to lower your premium by providing your driving record to your insurance company. About Your TN Driving Record In Tennessee, your MVR shows your complete driving history for the last 3 years. Your official TN driving record will typically include information on your: TN driver's license revocations and suspensions. Accidents and/or points held against you. Traffic violations, citations, and fines. TN driver's license endorsements and classifications. Who Can See Your Driving Record? Other people can order your TN driving record if their request falls into any of the following categories: Legal requirements. Employment background checks. Fraud investigation. Government business. Insurance. Statistical research. Law enforcement. Part of a private investigation. If you need to order another TN resident's driving record, see the “ Employer, Attorney, and Insurance Requests" section below. Order Your Tennessee Driving Record You can order your driving record in Tennessee online, in person, or by mail. Online To order your TN driving record online, visit the TN Department of Safety website and provide: Your last name and date of birth. Your TN driver's license number. Payment for the $5 fee. See the “Fees for a TN Driving Record" section below. You will be provided with a PDF version of your Tennessee record that you can either save or print. NOTE: If you have previously blocked access to your TN driving record, you will need to order your driving record by mail or in person. In Person To order your MVR in person, visit a Tennessee Driver Service Center and provide: Your name and date of birth. Your TN driver's license number. Payment for the $5 fee. See the “Fees for a TN Driving Record" section below. NOTE: If you have previously blocked access to your TN driving record, you will also need to provide a completed Request for Record Individual Authorization (Form SF-1282). If you can't make it to a TN Driver Service Center in person, you can either: Have another person order it for you in person. They must provide the TN DMV with a notarized statement of authorization from you. OR Request your TN driving record by mail (see below). By Mail To order your Tennessee driving record by mail, write a request that includes your: Name. Date of birth. Tennessee driver's license number. NOTE: If you have previously blocked access to your TN driving record, you will also need to provide a completed Request for Record Individual Authorization (Form SF-1282). Send your request and payment for the $5 fee (see the “Fees for a TN Driving Record" section below) to: Tennessee Department of Safety and Homeland Security MVR Request P.O. Box 945 Nashville, TN 37202 Please allow 2 weeks from the mailing date for the TN DMV to send your driving record. Employer, Attorney, and Insurance Requests You can order someone else's TN driving record if your request is an authorized purpose as defined by the Driver's Privacy Protection Act (DPPA). If you are eligible to order another person's TN driving record, you will need: A completed Request for Record Authorized Requester (Form SF-1279). Verification of the request on company letterhead. Payment for the $5 fee. See the “Fees for a TN Driving Record" section below. Fees for a TN Driving Record The fee for an MVR will depend on whether you ordered it online, by mail, or in person. Online: $5. By mail: $5. In person: $5. Accepted Payment Methods When ordering a TN driving record online, payment must be made by one of the following credit/debit cards: VISA. MasterCard. American Express. Discover. For requests by mail, payment must be made by cashiers check or money order made payable to “Tennessee Department of Safety and Homeland Security." If you're ordering your MVR in person, TN DMV offices accept payment by: Cash. Check or money order made payable to “Tennessee Department of Safety and Homeland Security." VISA, MasterCard, and Discover credit cards. Correct Your TN Driving Record If you need to correct an error on your MVR, contact the Tennessee Department of Safety at: (615) 253-5221. OR (866) 849-3548 (toll free).

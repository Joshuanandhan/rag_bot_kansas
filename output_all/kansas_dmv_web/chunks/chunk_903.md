On-Premise License Caterer License: Allows the licensee to offer for sale, sell and serve alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage for consumption on unlicensed premises for limited durations at catered events, which may be open to the public. Class A Club: Allows the licensee to offer for sale, sell and serve alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage for consumption on the licensed premises by members and their families and guests accompanying them; offer for sale, sell and serve alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage to non-members during specified events; make sales of alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage for consumption off of the licensed premises in accordance with K.S.A. 41-2653; serve free samples of alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage on the licensed premises. Class B Club: Allows the licensee to offer for sale, sell and serve alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage for consumption on the licensed premises by members and guests accompanying them; make sales of alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage for consumption off of the licensed premises in accordance with K.S.A. 41-2653; serve free samples of alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage on the licensed premises. Drinking Establishment: Allows the licensee to sell and serve alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage for consumption on the licensed premises and other activities as authorized by K.S.A. 41-2642, and sell and serve alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage for consumption off of the licensed premises as authorized by K.S.A. 41-2653; serve free samples of alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage on the licensed premises. Public Venue License: Allows the licensee to offer for sale, sell and serve alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage for consumption on the licensed premise of an arena, stadium, hall or theater. The on-premise licenses types are Caterer, Class A Club, Class B Club, Drinking Establishment, Drinking Establishment/Caterer, Hotel/Drinking Establishment, Hotel Drinking Establishment/Caterer and Public Venue licenses. If your event is temporary, visit the temporary permit page.

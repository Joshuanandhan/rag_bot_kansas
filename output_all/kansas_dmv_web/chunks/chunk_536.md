DMV.ORG Named One of the Best Places to Work by San Diego Business Journal ENCINITAS, CA--(June 20, 2016) - DMV.ORG, the nation's largest independent resource for DMV-related information and services, today announced that it was named as one of the Best Places to Work in San Diego in the small-sized company category for 2016 by the San Diego Business Journal. Honorees are made up of 100 San Diego-based companies from four categories; small, medium, large, and mega-sized companies. Businesses are recognized for their workplace policies, practices, philosophy, systems, demographics, and employee experiences. Best Companies Group managed the overall registration and survey process in San Diego, analyzed the data, and determined the final rankings. Bob Kurilko, President & Chief Operating Officer at DMV.ORG, said, “We're aiming to redefine what a company can truly be in the 21st century, and this starts with building a high-performance culture founded on great team care, high standards, and driven by a meaningful purpose. Great people define great companies, and I couldn't be prouder of the team we've assembled here at DMV.ORG; our integrated and high-functioning team makes our company not only a great place to work, but makes www.dmv.org one of the best sites on the web." "We're thrilled to be recognized by the San Diego business community for what we have to offer to our employees. This includes our team wellness, mindfulness, and catered lunch programs, team training, and corporate responsibility initiatives," said Catherine Linamen, HR Talent Manager. “But it's our employees who truly make DMV.ORG one of the best places to work in San Diego." Companies that made the 2016 Best Places to Work in San Diego list will be honored at an awards ceremony on August 11, 2016. Honorees will also be featured in a special issue of the San Diego Business Journal, scheduled for publication on August 22, 2016. Learn more about DMV.ORG at http://www.dmv.org/our-company/. You can also visit www.BestPlacestoWorkSD.com for more information on the Best Places to Work in San Diego program. ABOUT DMV.ORG With over 180 million annual visitors, DMV.ORG is the nation's largest independent resource for DMV-related information, products, and services. Since 1999, DMV.ORG has organized, simplified, and worked to redefine vehicle drivers' relationship with the complex world of the DMV and motor vehicle compliance. With driver's tests, form libraries, insurance, registration, DMV office finders, and industry-leading partner services, the company's digital publishing house empowers drivers with what they need to reduce their time in line at their state agency and free up more time in their lives. DMV.ORG is a privately-owned digital publishing house located in Encinitas, California. Learn more: www.dmv.org.

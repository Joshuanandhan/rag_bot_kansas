To obtain a Kansas CDL, you must visit the driver’s license office to complete the required written exams for the class of license needed, air brakes, and any endorsements you will need to obtain a CDL permit. You may visit https://ksrevenue.gov/pdf/cdlhandbook.pdf for a PDF of the handbook to study for the exam. Alternatively, a printed copy may be purchased from the driver’s license office for $2.00. Page 28 in the attached handbook will cover which sections of the book you need to study for your class of license and endorsements. You must then hold your permit for 14 days or more, then complete a skills exam in the vehicle for which you will be driving. You will be required to complete the Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) to obtain a Kansas CDL. Please visit Training Provider Registry (dot.gov) for more information on the ELDT training. You may schedule your CDL driving exam once your permit is received and your ELDT is complete, it would just have to be scheduled for 14 or more days from the date your permit was issued. You must call or email one of the offices on page 6 in the attached handbook to schedule a CDL drive.

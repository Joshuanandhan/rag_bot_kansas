Microdistillery License Microdistillery License: Allows the manufacturer and storage of not more than 50,000 gallons of spirits per year. The sale of spirits manufactured by the licensee in the original unopened container to be consumed off the licensed premise. May provide free samples on the licensed premise and at special events monitored and regulated by the ABC.C. Microdistillery Packaging and Warehousing Facility: Allows existing microdistillery licensees to move products from their manufacturing premise to a separate facility for packaging and/or storage and delivery to a licensed spirits distributor. A licensed microdistillery may also apply for a microdistillery packaging and warehousing facility, drinking establishment or caterer's license.

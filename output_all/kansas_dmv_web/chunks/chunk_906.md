Distributor License Distributor: Allows the licensee to make the wholesale purchase, importation and storage of alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage and the sale to licensees as authorized by K.S.A. 41-306, K.S.A. 41-306a and K.S.A. 41-307. There are three types of distributor licenses: Beer Distributor Spirits Distributor Wine Distributor All distributors must file their gallonage tax return, purchase report, sales report and remit their payment by the 15th of the month following their purchases and sales. Distributors have the option to use the conventional method filing paper forms or file using Electronic Data Interchange (EDI). Distributors must also report monthly sales to on-premise licensees.

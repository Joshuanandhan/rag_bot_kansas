Cartography The Cartography Section's responsibilities include: Assist counties, taxpayers and others with cadastral mapping issues; Create and Maintain Geographic Information System (GIS); Provide educational workshops and courses related to GIS and Property Ownership Mapping; Supply thematic maps to PVD associates and others; Participate in state GIS activities and coordination.

(TB-144) A tax is imposed upon the privilege of selling or dealing in tobacco products in this state by any person engaged in the business as a distributor thereof, at the rate of ten percent (10%) of the wholesale sales price of such tobacco products. The tax will be imposed at the time when the distributor (a) brings or causes to be brought into this state from without the state tobacco products for sale; (b) makes, manufactures, or fabricates tobacco products in this state for sale in this state; or (c) ships or transports tobacco products to retailers in this state to be sold by those retailers.

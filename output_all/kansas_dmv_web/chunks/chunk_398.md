LINE 40 (INTEREST): If you paid your tax after the original due date, compute interest at the rate of .75% per month (or fraction thereof) on the balance due and enter the result on line 40. LINE 41 (PENALTY): If you paid your tax after the original due date, compute the penalty at 1% for each month (or portion thereof) the return is late or the tax is unpaid on the balance due amount, up to a maximum of 24%.

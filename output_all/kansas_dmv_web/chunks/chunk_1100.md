Driving Records in Indiana You can order your IN driving record through the Indiana BMV: Online through the BMV's online request portal. By mail or in person using the BMV's record request form. For further details, including required forms and fees, keep reading below!Driving Records in Indiana You can order your Indiana driving record through the IN Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV). Your driving record contains your personal information and driving history, and can be used by: Employers for background checks. Government agencies. Insurance companies to adjust policy rates and investigate claims. Types of Indiana Driving Records The Indiana BMV offers: Viewable driver records (VDR): Available online for free. Official driving records (ODR): Includes a certification letter and can be used for official purposes. Your IN driving record contains your: Full name. Home address. Date of birth. Gender. Driver's license number, class, and expiration date. Driver's license status. Driving record points. Traffic violations and convictions. Social Security number (SSN). Physical description. Driving restrictions. You can order your own ODR or VDR driving record: Online. By mail. In person. You can request another Indiana driver's ODR by mail or in person only. Employer & Third-Party Requests You may be able to request someone else's Indiana BMV driving record if you are any of the following: Attorney. Government agency, official, or employee. Law enforcement officer. Parent or legal guardian. Employers conducting a background check. To request someone else's driver record by mail or in person, you will need: A completed Request for Driver Records (Form 53789). Payment for the $4 fee. Take your form and payment to your local Indiana BMV office or mail them to the address specified on the form. NOTE: If requesting the driving record by mail, payment must be made by check or money order payable to “BMV." Order your non-certified Indiana driving record online! Order it online today.Order Your Own Indiana Driving Record In Indiana, you can order your driving record either online, by mail, or in person.Online To request your IN driving record online, you'll need an IN BMV online account. If you haven't previously created one, register by providing: Your IN driver's license number. The first 5 digits of your zip code. The last 4 digits of your Social Security number (SSN). Once you've registered, log into your account and follow the instructions. If you want your full official driving record, you will need to provide an e-check, credit, or debit card for the $4 fee. A viewable driver record is free.By Mail & In Person To request your Indiana BMV driving record by mail or in person, you'll need: A completed Request for Driver Records (Form 53789). Payment for the $4 fee. Take your form and payment to your local Indiana BMV office or mail them to the address specified on the form. NOTE: If requesting the driving record by mail, payment must be made by check or money order payable to “BMV."Correct Your IN Driving Record If you believe there is an error on your Indiana driving record, you should contact the IN Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) at (888) 692-6841. It is important to check that all information on your Indiana driving record is accurate. Incorrect information on your driving record could lead to an increase in your insurance premiums, fines, and even license suspension.

TIRE EXCISE TAX RECORDS Like any part of your business operation, complete and accurate records of tire and vehicle sales must be kept for at least three prior years. For Kansas tire excise tax purposes, your records should have the following information and documents. All invoices of new tire sales and/or sales of new or used vehicles. All exempt sales allowed by law. All exemption certificates. A true, complete inventory taken at least once a year. Your records of retail sales and tire excise tax must be available for, and are subject to, inspection by the Director of Taxation (or authorized representative).

Tax Unit Boundary Certifications by County The tax unit boundary certifications list changes to the tax units within each county. Certifications are deleted every January. New certifications will be available as they are received by the Division of Property Valuation from each county. If you have questions about a county's certification, contact the county clerk at the telephone number on the county's certification.

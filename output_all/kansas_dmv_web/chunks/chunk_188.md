Escrow Payment If your account has enough money in your escrow account, you can use that amount to apply to your invoice total. You will select “Escrow” Under “Payment Type”, the escrow amount will then be applied. If the amount covers the invoice total, then press “Proceed”. If the amount is less than the total, then you would press “Add” this will add a second line to Payment Type. You will select “Escrow” for “Payment Type” and enter the remaining balance under “Payment Amount”. Select “Proceed”, Confirm, and then “Complete”. Then the system will generate a Payment Receipt. Credentials To receive your plate and cab card, documentation will have to be sent to or taken into a preferred county location. There they will verify that the paperwork and documentation is filled out correctly and then complete the transaction from an internal standpoint.

Teacher’s Purchases of School and Classroom Supplies Tax Credit INCOME TAXPAYERS – Effective for tax year 2022 and all tax years thereafter. An income tax credit shall be allowed in an amount equal to the expenditures made by the taxpayer for school and classroom supplies during the taxable year. Qualifications A tax credit shall be allowed for an individual who is a Kansas resident and is employed as a public or private school teacher. Credit Amount The credit amount cannot exceed $250 per teacher or $500 for two teachers who are married filing jointly. Procedures to Claim the Credit You may download Schedule K-91 to claim the Teacher’s Purchases of School and Classroom Supplies tax credit. This schedule must be completed and submitted with the income return. You may also call the Department of Revenue voice mail system at 785-296-4937 to request Schedule K-91. You will be asked to give your name, address, phone number and form(s) you desire. Please allow two weeks for delivery of your forms. Back to Tax Credits page.

Vehicle Registration After you buy a car, you'll need to register it with your local Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV), Secretary of State (SOS), Department of Revenue (DOR), Motor Vehicle Division (MVD), or other local entity that sets vehicle regulations in your state. If someone is asking for your vehicle registration number, they are probably referring to your Vehicle Identification (VIN) number. Before you can register your car, your vehicle will likely need to: Have a title in your name. Pass an emissions test or smog check. Pass a vehicle safety inspection. Be covered by car insurance.

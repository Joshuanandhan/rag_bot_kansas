Contractor Workshops The Kansas Department of Revenue offers periodic Sales Tax workshops for construction contractor, subcontractors and repairmen conducted at various locations across the state. The workshops are devoted to the sales tax issues for construction contractors. These specialized seminars are directed toward those who provide plumbing, carpentry, electrical, roofing, HVAC, and landscaping. etc. services. Topics include; how sales tax applies to material and labor, Project Exemption Certificates, sales tax returns, billing related issues. To register: Please call the sponsoring Small Business Development Center (SBDC). Some of the SBDCs charge a fee for attending the Contractor Workshops.

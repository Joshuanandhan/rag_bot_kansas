Packaging and Warehousing Facility Permit Packaging and Warehousing Facility Permit: Allows any out-of-state or Kansas manufacturer or supplier to transfer alcoholic liquor or cereal malt beverage to the licensed premises for packaging or storage or both; the sale and transfer of alcoholic liquor or cereal malt beverage from the licensed premises to a Kansas distributor or supplier; or, the transfer from the licensed premises to another state. Licensee Information Brand Registration and Label Approval: Each brand and label of alcoholic liquor or cereal malt beverage that is intended for sale to distributors in Kansas and is transported, packaged or stored at a packaging and warehousing facility must be registered with the ABC by the supplier or manufacturer prior to shipment to the licensed Kansas distributor. Gallonage Taxes and Reports: The gallonage tax shall be paid on alcoholic liquor or cereal malt beverage imported into this state under a packaging and warehousing facility permit only if the alcoholic liquor or cereal malt beverage is sold to a Kansas distributor for sale at wholesale in this state. The gallonage tax shall be paid by the Kansas distributor who purchases the alcoholic liquor or cereal malt beverage for sale at wholesale. Packaging and Warehousing Facility permit holders are not required to file any monthly or annual gallonage tax returns or reports.

LINE 31 (Interest): Using the amount on line 30, compute interest at .333% for each month (or fraction thereof) from the original due date of the return. LINE 32 (Penalty): Using the amount on line 30, compute penalty at 1% per month (or fraction thereof) from the original due date of the return. The maximum penalty is 24%. LINE 33 (Estimated tax penalty): An estimated tax penalty may be due if the total of your withholding and estimated tax payments (lines 22 and 23) subtracted from line 19 is $500 or more.

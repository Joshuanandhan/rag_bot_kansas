Producer License Producer License: Allows the licensee to sell, either in the original unopened container or by the drink on the licensed premises, wine produced in whole or in part using grapes, fruits, berries or honey grown or produced by the licensee. Such wine must be manufactured by a licensed farm winery and purchased by the licensee. May also provide wine tastings on the licensed premises. Licensee Information Handbook for Producers Each label must be registered by the owner of the label. Application Prerequisites New applicants are required to obtain a Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) prior to submitting your application for liquor licensure if they do not already possess their FEIN. For more information, go to: http://www.irs.gov/ You must register for business taxes. Choose either the online option or paper form below.

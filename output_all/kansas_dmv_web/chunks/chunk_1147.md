To order your WY driving record through the Wyoming DOT: Mail in your completed record request along with payment for the record fee. Visit your local driver examination office in person with your completed form and fee. For further details, keep reading below!Driving Records in Wyoming Whether you need your own driving record, or you need to review the driving record of another driver, the Wyoming Department of Transportation (DOT) allows for multiple ways to request a driver's history. Read more to learn about Wyoming driving records and how to order your own or order someone else's record.Driving Record Information Your Wyoming driving record will have information from the past 3 years to 5 years, including: From the past 3 years: Moving violations. Uninsured accidents. Violations of car insurance laws. Traffic violations in other states. Driver's license suspensions. From the past 5 years: DUIs. Reckless driving. Accident judgments. Vehicular homicide. Leaving the scene of an accident involving an injury. Any felony resulting from driving. Transporting alcohol to minors. NOTE: You may also request a 10-year driving history. Ordering Your Own Driver Record In Wyoming you can order your driving record: In person. By Mail. Each driving record you order costs $10.In Person To request your driving record in person, visit any WY driver examination office with: Your Wyoming driver's license OR ID card. Payment. While all offices accept cash and checks, not all offices accept credit cards. Call ahead to verify your local office's accepted payment methods. You will receive your driving record immediately.By Mail To order your Wyoming driving record by mail, send a check or money order payable to “WYDOT" for your fee payment along with: A completed Release for Driving Record & Personal Information (Form DSFR-11e). OR A letter of request containing your: Full name. Date of birth. Social Security number OR WY driver's license number. Mail the above to: Wyoming Department of Transportation Driver Service - Driving Records 5300 Bishop Blvd Cheyenne, WY 82009 Your driving record request should be processed within 10 business days.Ordering Another Driver's WY Driving Record Under the Federal Driver Privacy Protection Act, the DOT will release your driving record to (but not limited to) the following: Research companies. Civil, criminal, and administrative courts. Anyone with written permission from you. Any person or agency in conjunction with driver safety, recalls, theft, emissions, or vehicle alterations. Private investigation agencies. While all of the above can access your driving record, the most common requests for driving records come from: Employers – To help with hiring decisions. Insurance companies – To help determine car insurance premiums. If you are compliant with the Driver Privacy Protection Act, you can request a driver's history for 3 years, 5 years, or a 10-year by completing a Release for Driving Record & Personal Information (Form DSFR-11e) with: Your: Name or company name. Phone number. Address. The driver's: Name. Date of birth. Social Security number OR WY driver's license number. A check or money order for fee payment (government agencies are exempt). If you do not have permission to see an individual's driving record as detailed through the Driver Privacy Protection Act, you must provide the driver's written consent on the Release for Driving Record & Personal Information (Form DSFR-11e) form. Mail the above to: Wyoming Department of Transportation Driver Service - Driving Records 5300 Bishop Blvd Cheyenne, WY 82009 Allow 10 business days for your order to process. NOTE: You must have an Agreement & Contract with the DOT in order to resell or re-distribute personal information obtained from a third party. To obtain this, a detailed written request must be sent to: Wyoming Department of Transportation, Driver Services Attn: Marianne Zivkovich 5300 Bishop Blvd Cheyenne, WY 82009Correcting Information on WY Driving Records Because your driving record can greatly affect your life in many ways, it's always a good idea to periodically check it for errors. If you suspect that the information on your Wyoming driving record is incorrect, contact the DOT's customer service desk at (307) 777-4800. When calling, be prepared to provide your driver's license number or Social Security number.

Special Order Shipping License Special Order Shipping License: Allows the winery to ship wine upon order directly to Kansas consumers for personal or household use. The special order shipping license is available to any maker or producer of wine whether in this state or in any other state who holds a valid federal basic wine manufacturing permit. Authorized to ship wine upon order directly to Kansas consumers for personal or household use. May use the services of a Kansas licensed fulfillment house to warehouse and package its wine and process and ship orders for such wine to consumers.

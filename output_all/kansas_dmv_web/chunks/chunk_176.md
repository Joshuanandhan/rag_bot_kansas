Welcome to CMV/IRP Service Provider Training This training is designed for third party vendors conducting CMV/IRP transactions for companies using the Kansas Commercial Vehicle Registration System (KCoVRS). We will provide you with some background information on transactions done at the county/state level and there will be an emphasis on correct documentation to be collected for each transaction you do as a vendor. Objectives: Documentation County/State Transactions Add Vehicle Supplement CMV Renewal IRP Renewal Miscellaneous Tasks Participant Guide – this has been e-mailed to you so you may follow along and use the guide as reference when performing transactions in the KCoVRS system. Documentation Reference Guide – this has been provided to you as a checklist to ensure you are collecting all the correct documentation. You may use these as a cover sheet when collecting and submitting documentation. Summary Exercise – at the end of each module, you will take a short quiz. You must score 80% or better to pass the module and be able to move on. If you do not pass, you will be able to retake the quiz to obtain a passing score. Once you have passed all modules you will be e-mailed your score and mailed your certificate.

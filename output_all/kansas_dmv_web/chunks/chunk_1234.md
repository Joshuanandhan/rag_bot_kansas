Organ Donation in New Mexico Organ Donation in New Mexico With the help of the New Mexico Donor Services, Donate Life New Mexico supports and promotes all efforts to raise the number of organ donor registrations in New Mexico. More than 105,000 people are waiting for an organ transplant in the United States. Keep in mind, there are absolutely no fees for registration or the actual transplant. Sign up as an organ donor TODAY to give the gift of life to others after you pass. Register as an Organ Donor in New Mexico You can join the New Mexico organ donor registry online, or in person at your local NM MVD office. Online To register online, you'll need to complete the online registration form at the Donor Services website. You can also add your information to ORGANIZE.org's central database to both register in New Mexico AND have your registration follow you from state to state. In Person When applying for or renewing a driver's license or ID card, you can register as an organ donor. You'll be given a red heart sticker to affix to your driver's license or ID card. Those who already have a license or ID card may add their choice to become a donor by picking up a sticker from any MVD field office. You may also indicate your donor decision by completing a Uniform Donor Card and keeping it in your wallet or purse. The Benefits of Organ Donation In New Mexico, there is a severe shortage or organ donors, particularly within the minority population. A new name is added to the national organ transplant waiting list approximately every 8 minutes. Thousands die each year due to the lack of organ donors in America. The good news is you can save the lives of up to 8 people as an organ donor. Signing up will offer hope to all those in need. There are all kinds of misconceptions about organ donation. Read more about the topic at the following pages so you can share this information with your friends and family: How Organ Donation Works Organ Donation Facts Organ Donation Myths Donation Facts A single donor can benefit dozens of patients. Becoming a donor will NOT cost your family anything. Almost anyone can become a donor, including the elderly and those with medical problems. All major religious organizations within the United States support organ and tissue donations. Organ donation will not alter or delay funeral plans; an open casket is still possible. For additional facts and statistics about organ donation, please visit our Organ Donation Facts page. How You Can Help With Organ Donation After you register as an organ donor in New Mexico, you can support the cause by: Volunteering with Donate Life NM. Make a monetary donation to Donor Services. Encourage your friends and family to register as an organ donor. Change Your Organ Donor Profile To update your organ donor profile online, you can visit the Donor Services New Mexico website, or ORGANIZE.org's central database. The New Mexico Donor Services, headquartered in Albuquerque, handles organ and tissue donations within the state.

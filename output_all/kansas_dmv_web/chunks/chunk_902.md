Retailer License Retailer's License: Allows the licensee to serve free samples of alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage; make retail sales of alcoholic liquor, cereal malt beverage, cigarette and tobacco products (with additional licensure), and all other goods and services; deliver alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage in the original package to certain other licensees and make other sales as authorized by K.S.A. 41-308. Sales of kegs must be tagged. See the “Keg Registration Forms” section below for details. Retailers that also wholesale to on-premise licensees, temporary permit holders or cereal malt beverage retailers must also file a monthly report of sales. Licensee Information Handbook for Retailers Application Prerequisites New applicants are required to obtain a Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) prior to submitting your application for liquor licensure if they do not already possess their FEIN. For more information, go to: http://www.irs.gov/ You must register for business taxes. Choose either the online option or paper form below.

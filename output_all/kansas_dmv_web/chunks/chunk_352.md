If the basket contains anything other than food and food ingredients, the basket does not qualify for the reduced state sales tax rate, and instead is subject to sales tax at the full state rate. This is because all the items, including the basket or box, are sold together as part of a “single item” and the highest rate of tax imposed on any part of the basket or box is applied to the entire basket or box. EXAMPLE: A shop or store sells a “food” basket or box that contains crackers, several types of cheese, and a summer sausage, all of which are produced by a manufacturer. The basket or box itself is for a single use and is disposable. All the items in the basket or box are food and food ingredients subject to the reduced state sales tax rate because they are not heated by the seller, they are not a mixture or combination of two or more food and food ingredients prepared by the seller and sold as a single item, and the seller does not provide eating utensils. As a result, the basket or box is sold at the reduced rate. SPECIAL SITUATIONS STORE SAMPLES When a business donates, gives away, or otherwise personally uses merchandise from its inventory, the business becomes the final consumer and is required to pay sales tax on its cost for the item, not the retail price of the item. When a business that sells food products removes food products from its inventory for use as store samples, it will pay tax on its cost for the food products at the reduced state sales tax rate. This is true regardless of whether the business heats, mixes or combines, or provides utensils for use with the food products it offers as store samples. MEAL KITS / MEAL SERVICES A meal kit service is any service that sends preportioned, fresh ingredients and recipes to customers. The customer combines and cooks these ingredients to prepare a meal. These meal kits are taxed at the reduced rate as food and food ingredients. In contrast, meal delivery services offer a range of prepackaged meals ready to reheat and eat by the customer. These meals are two or more ingredients, mixed or combined by the seller, for sale as a single item. These meals are taxed at the full state sales tax rate.

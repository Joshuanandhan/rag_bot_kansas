NOTE REGARDING SEARCHES: The Kansas Department of Revenue has made some changes to our Policy Library. Specifically, we are now linking you to official sites for some document types. This includes Kansas Statutes, Kansas Administrative Regulations, Kansas Session Laws, Constitutional Articles and Board of Tax Appeal Orders. As such, the search function associated with this Policy Information Library will only search the documents that are located within our library. The search function within this library will not search the other officials sites we are linking you to for those particular document types.

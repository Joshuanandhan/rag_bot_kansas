Q-Flow Wait Line Management System Get in line online and skip the waiting room. There are two options to get in line at the driver’s license offices in Andover, Derby, Lawrence, Manhattan, Mission, Olathe, Overland Park, Topeka, Wichita, and Wyandotte. The first is to check in line at the driver’s license office using our touch-screen monitors as soon as you arrive. The second option is to get in the virtual mobile line using your phone, computer or mobile device. Please note if you use the virtual option you must check into the office when you arrive to officially be in line. Because of this, if the office reaches capacity before you arrive and are checked in, you may not be served that day. The system will send you one email or text letting you know that you have placed your virtual ticket. For the location and hours of each office, please click on the city closest to you.

Biodiesel is subject to the Environmental Assurance Fee. K.S.A. 65-34,102. Definitions. As used in the Kansas storage tank act: (l) "Petroleum" means petroleum, including crude oil or any fraction thereof, which is liquid at standard conditions of temperature and pressure, 60 degrees Fahrenheit and 14.7 pound per square inch absolute, including, but not limited to, gasoline, gasohol, diesel fuel, fuel oils, kerosene and biofuels.

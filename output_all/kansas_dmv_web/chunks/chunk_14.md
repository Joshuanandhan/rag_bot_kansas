Our Company Putting you in the driver's seat At DMV.ORG, we want to make your life simpler. That's why we've dedicated the last 20+ years to helping you navigate your local DMV agency without hassle or fuss. We simplify complicated DMV information and offer easy online alternatives, so you can access what you need from one convenient place. We're NOT your state agency. Our goal is to complement the state agencies. We believe that by helping you find the forms, facts, and information you need, we lessen the stress and strain on state DMV agencies. We aim to shorten lines, reduce calls, and increase the number of informed consumers at the DMV office—which helps everyone stay happy. We provide accurate, up-to-date content. Our researchers and writers constantly review the state DMV sites for any changes and immediately update our site to reflect the latest regulations. Our proprietary Fact Check system notifies us when something on our website is out of date, so you know you're getting the most accurate information available. We operate on the “Do Good" rule. DMV.ORG has a unique view of how a company should operate. It's our belief that by doing good, we do better as a company, an employer, and as individuals. We're committed to making a positive impact on this world with information on clean transportation, organ donation, “green" driving initiatives, and vital safety tips for reduced auto accidents.

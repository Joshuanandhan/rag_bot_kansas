Property Valuation Abstract Section The Abstract Section of the Division of Property Valuation establishes the uniform reporting of value, taxes and levies from the county officials to the Division. The reporting of the value, taxes and levies by the Director of the Division of Property of Valuation to the Governor and the members of the Legislature each year is required by K.S.A. 79-1806 and 79-1404, Fourteenth. The section also compiles and publishes the information received on statewide value, tax and levy annually.

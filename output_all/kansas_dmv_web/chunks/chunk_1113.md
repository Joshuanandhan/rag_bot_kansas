Driving Records in Missouri Driving Records in Missouri Whether you need your own driving record, or the history of another Missouri resident, you can order it through the MO Department of Revenue (DOR) Motor Vehicle and Driver Licensing Division. Your Missouri driving record contains information about your MO driving history and can be used: To adjust your car insurance policy rates and investigate claims. For pre-employment screenings and background checks. In federal, state, and local courts, or DMV agency proceedings and hearings. On this page you'll find information on how to request your driving record by mail, by fax/e-mail, and in person. Types of Missouri Driving Records The Missouri DMV offers driving records: Without personal information, which contain the driver's: Traffic violations. Traffic accidents. Driving history. With personal information, which contain the driver's: Traffic violations. Traffic accidents. Driving history. Name. Address. Date of birth. Physical description. MO driver's license number. Social Security number (SSN). Photograph. Phone number. Medical and disability restrictions and information. Driving Record Points When you violate traffic laws or cause accidents, the Missouri Department of Revenue (DOR) will add points to your driving record. Points on your driving record can lead to fines, driver's license suspensions, cancellations, and increased insurance rates. To find out how many points are on your driving record, contact the Missouri DMV: Phone: (573) 526-2407. E-mail: dlbmail@dor.mo.gov. Employer, Insurance Agency, & Attorney Requests You can request another person's Missouri driving record if your request is for one of the following purposes: Government agencies conducting official business. For use in a civil or criminal legal proceeding in a local, state, or federal court. An insurance agency investigating claims or adjusting policy rates. Conducting a background check on a current or potential employee. You can request driving records without personal information by mail or in person. To request Missouri driving records that contain personal information, you must request a MO Department of Revenue security access code. (See the “Driving Record With Personal Information" section below.) Driving Record Without Personal Information By Mail To request a driving record without personal information by mail, you'll need: A written request that includes: The driver's driver license number, date of birth, and full name. Your mailing address. Payment. (See the “Fees for MO Driving Records" section below.) Mail your request and payment to: Motor Vehicle and Driver Licensing Division 301 West High Street Room 470 Jefferson City, MO 65101 In Person To request a driving record without personal information in person, visit a Missouri DMV office and: Provide the MO driver's license number, birth date and full name of the driver. Pay the driving record fee. (See the “Fees for MO Driving Records" section below.) Driving Record With Personal Information If you need to request a driving record that includes personal information, you'll need to complete a Request for Security Access Code (Form DOR-4678). Mail the form to the address located at the top of the application. Once your application is approved, the Missouri DOR will assign you a security access code to use when requesting driving records with personal information. Order it online today. How to Get Your Own Missouri Driving Record Order Your Driving Record By Mail To request your own driving record by mail: Complete a Request From Record Holder (Form DOR-4681). Your form must include your signature and a notary's signature. Include your driving record fee. (See “Fees for MO Driving Records" below.) Mail your documents to: Motor Vehicle and Driver Licensing Division 301 West High Street Room 470 Jefferson City, MO 65101 In Person To request your own MO driving record in person, visit your local Missouri DMV and: Complete a Request From Record Holder (Form DOR-4681). Your form must include your signature and a notary's signature. Pay the fee. (See the “Fees for MO Driving Records" section below.) By Fax or E-mail To request your own Missouri driving record by fax or e-mail: Complete a Request From Record Holder (Form DOR-4681). Your form must include your signature and a notary's signature. Complete the credit/debit card authorization section on the form to pay the fee. (See the “Fees for MO Driving Records" section below.) Fax your form to (573) 526-7367, or e-mail it to dlrecords@dor.mo.gov. Fees for MO Driving Records The following fees apply when ordering a driving record in Missouri: Driving record: $2.82. Additional fee if ordered by fax: $.50. Additional fee if ordered in person: $2. Acceptable Forms of Payment For driving record transactions in person, the MO DMV accepts: Cash. Check. Money order. VISA, Discover, MasterCard, and American Express debit/credit card. For transactions by mail, the Missouri DMV accepts: Check. Money order. VISA, Discover, MasterCard, and American Express debit/credit card. For driving records requested by fax or e-mail, you can pay with Visa, Discover, MasterCard and American Express debit/credit card.

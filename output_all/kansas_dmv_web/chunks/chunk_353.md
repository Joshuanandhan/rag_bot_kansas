NICOTINE GUM Nicotine gum is an FDA-approved product that can help people quit smoking. Nicotine gum will be taxed at the full state sales tax rate because it does not meet the definition of food and food ingredients. While nicotine gum is chewed, it is not consumed for its taste or nutritional value, and it is not considered candy or a dietary supplement. CBD PRODUCTS Cannabidiol (CBD) is a product that is derived from the cannabis plant which comes in various forms, including, oils and tinctures, edibles, capsules, topicals, and vape juices. CBD products will be taxed at the full state sales tax rate because they do not meet the definition of food and food ingredients. Any non-food CBD products are not food and food ingredients. CBD products in the form of vape juices are inhaled and not ingested, and so are not food and food ingredients. Edible forms of CBD are not consumed for their taste nor nutritional value, but rather for their effects, so they are not food and food ingredients. Lastly, according to the Federal Food and Drug Administration, it is currently illegal to market CBD by adding it to a food or labeling it as a dietary supplement, thus is not a food and food ingredient. The retailer in this example has made $100,000 in gross sales of items that do not qualify for the reduction, reported on row 1 in the normal sales jurisdiction.

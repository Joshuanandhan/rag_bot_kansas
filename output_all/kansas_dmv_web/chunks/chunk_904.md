Common Consumption Area Permit Common Consumption Area Permit: Allows the consumption of alcoholic liquor and cereal malt beverage in the area designated by such permit. Licensee Information Request to Participate in Common Consumption Area (ABC-838) Common Consumption Area Permit Handbook Application Prerequisites New applicants are required to obtain a Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) prior to submitting your application for liquor licensure if they do not already possess their FEIN. For more information, go to: http://www.irs.gov/ City or county must establish a common consumption area by ordinance or resolution authorizing the possession and consumption of alcoholic liquor within the common consumption area.

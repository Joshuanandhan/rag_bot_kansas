Driving Records in South Dakota A South Dakota motor vehicle record (MVR) – commonly known as a driving record – chronicles your driving history and reports on everything from traffic violations to driver license suspensions. You may obtain your driving record from the South Dakota Department of Public Safety (DPS). Below you'll find helpful information about driving records in South Dakota, including how a driving record can affect employment or how much you pay for car insurance in SD.Information on Your SD Driving Record South Dakota driving records provide details about your driving history, such as: Traffic accidents, violations or convictions. Driver license suspensions, revocations and cancellations. Points on your SD driver's license. Restrictions, if applicable (i.e., if you require glasses for driving). Your SD driving record reflects every traffic occurrence that takes place in or outside of the state within the past 3 years. If, for example, you get ticketed for speeding in Wyoming, your violation will appear on your driving record in South Dakota.Who Sees Your SD Driving Record Under the statutes of the Driver Privacy Protection Act (DPPA), your South Dakota driving record can be accessed by: Anyone with written permission from you. Auto insurance companies. Potential employers conducting background checks or employment screenings. Civil, criminal and administrative courts. Those serving notice to owners of towed or impounded vehicles. Most employers now include driving records as part of the pre-employment screening process, especially for commercial driver applicants.
